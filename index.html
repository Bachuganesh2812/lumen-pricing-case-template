08 / DATA ASSUMPTIONS

Know what the model cannot know.

No German LUMEN sales data

All historical sales are from NL/DK/SE. Germany is estimated from German survey intent, competitor pricing, channel economics and market context.

Customer privacy

Name/email-style survey fields are excluded from the frontend and all derived outputs. Only aggregate metrics and selected quotes are shown.

Model caveats

Price-test acceptance is survey-based, channel contributions are illustrative, and historical sales include intentional data-quality issues.
